Examine the infrastructure that supports organizational learning

The Organizational Learning Infrastructure Assessment (OLIA) is intended to help leaders reflect on how their organization detects change, captures knowledge, learns from experience, shares insights, and adapts.

It connects to the Organizational Learning Infrastructure framework and its ten learning domains.
