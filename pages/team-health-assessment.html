Reflect on the conditions that help your team coordinate, learn, adapt, and perform. You’ll see your results immediately after submitting the assessment.

About this assessment

Rate each statement based on your current experience. This is a reflection and learning tool—not a validated clinical or employment test. Scores are intended to support thoughtful discussion, not to label individuals or teams.

1 = Strongly disagree 2 = Disagree 3 = Neutral / unsure 4 = Agree 5 = Strongly agree

Avoid including names or confidential personnel details in open-ended responses. Your results are calculated in your browser. If Formspree is configured, the submitted answers are also sent to the assessment owner.

How to use these results

Look for patterns rather than treating a score as a verdict. Higher scores may point to conditions worth protecting and learning from; lower scores may identify areas for a team conversation. Consider the context behind each rating and compare perspectives respectfully.
