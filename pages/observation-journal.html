Observation Journal

A practical approach to noticing patterns, reflecting on experience, and connecting observations to larger ideas.

Practice

Observe with curiosity. Connect with purpose. Create with courage.

The Observation Journal is a practice for noticing what is happening before deciding what it means. It can help turn everyday experiences into questions, insights, and new connections.

Notice

What caught your attention today? Describe what happened before interpreting it.

Interpret

What patterns, assumptions, or competing explanations might help make sense of it?

Connect

What larger idea, theory, or experience does this observation bring to mind?

Explore

What question would you like to carry forward, test, or notice next time?

A simple daily entry

Observation: What did I notice?

Possible meaning: What might it suggest?

Connection: What other idea or experience does it connect to?

Question: What do I want to understand better?

This page is a starting point. It does not yet store journal entries or collect personal information.
